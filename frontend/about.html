How it works

1

Configure

Brand managers define tone, restricted words, and approved product facts.

2

Audit

Draft content is analysed against the rules. Each issue gets a severity, an explanation, and a purpose-preserving edit.

3

Human Review

A person accepts or rejects every suggestion. Nothing changes without explicit approval.

Responsible design principles NIST AI RMF-aligned

Govern

Guidelines are owned and editable by accountable brand managers, not hidden in model prompts.

Map

Issues are categorised (restricted word, factual mismatch, tone) so risks are visible and scoped.

Measure

Severity ratings (high, medium, low) let reviewers prioritise by potential impact.

Manage

Human-in-the-loop approval, transparent explanations, and non-deterministic model safety controls keep the system reliable.
