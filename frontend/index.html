Frequently Asked Questions

How does BrandTrace protect sensitive marketing drafts?
All drafts and guideline configurations are encrypted in transit and at rest. Role-based access control ensures only authorized organization members can view or edit brand rules.
Can we connect BrandTrace to our custom backend?
Yes! BrandTrace is fully integrated with a Flask backend supporting JWT authentication, SQLite storage, and modular REST endpoints for analysis, guidelines, and history.
What is the difference between High, Medium, and Low severity?
High severity flags legal or factual risks that block unreviewed publishing. Medium covers policy-adjacent wording, and Low addresses tone and style adjustments.
